About Me

I have been passionately developing video games for about two years. As a generalist, I work across the entire development pipeline: prototyping and gameplay implementation in Unreal Engine 5 and Unity, 3D modeling and texturing with Blender and Substance Painter, as well as version control with GitHub. For websites, I work with HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and Figma.
